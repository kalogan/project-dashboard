## The Ratio

A 1:2 brew ratio, pulled tight.

## Method

Grind fine, distribute, level, and tamp flat. If the shot runs under 25
seconds, grind finer; over 32, coarser. Adjust one variable at a time.

## Notes

Beans should rest 7–14 days off roast. Past three weeks, coarsen the grind to
compensate for the loss of CO₂.
