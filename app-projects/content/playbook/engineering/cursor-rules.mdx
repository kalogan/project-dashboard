The standing rules I load into every AI-assisted editing session. These are not
suggestions — they are the contract the agent operates under.

## Core Directives

- Match the surrounding code's style; do not introduce new conventions.
- Prefer the smallest change that fully solves the problem.
- Never invent APIs — verify against the actual source before calling.

## Review Posture

Every generated diff is treated as a junior engineer's PR: read it in full,
question anything clever, and reject net-new dependencies unless justified.

## Guardrails

The agent must stop and ask before deleting files, rewriting public interfaces,
or touching anything outside the stated task.

These rules were forged shipping [[exile-level-craft]] — every directive here
exists because something once went wrong without it.
